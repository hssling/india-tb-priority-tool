How to read this report

For each step of the TB care pathway, the tool asks how many patients would not have been lost if a State's loss rate were as good as the benchmark: the rate of the better quarter of States. This number is the harm averted. It is counted in patients and is shown with a 95% interval. For S1 it is the shortfall of notifications against the benchmark share of the target.

The tool then simulates 10,000 plausible versions of the data. P(top-5) is the share of these in which the State is among the 5 with the largest harm averted at that step. The tier is set from P(top-5):

0.20 ≤ P < 0.50

Caveats

  • A CTD notification target is a programme goal, not an estimate of disease. S1 is a shortfall against target: it does not count people with TB who were not found.
  • Harm averted counts patients lost at one step of the care pathway. Steps count different kinds of loss and are never added together; no combined score is formed.
  • With denominators of about 100,000 patients, P(top-5) mostly reflects binomial noise, so it is close to 0 or 1. The tiers therefore express little uncertainty beyond sampling (plan §10.4).
  • Harm averted is an absolute count, so large States have large values at every step. The plan expected the temporal-stability estimate (H1, outputs/headline.csv) to reflect unit size (plan §10.4); the size-free rate-gap correlation was similar, so the stability is not explained by size alone.
  • The association of the S1 shortfall with the National TB Prevalence Survey (H2) may be circular if CTD targets were set from burden estimates that include the survey (plan §10.4).
  • Small UTs are merged into the unit they adjoin (Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu into Gujarat, Lakshadweep into Kerala, Ladakh into Jammu and Kashmir) and are not prioritised separately.
  • The results describe programme data, not the performance or effort of any State's staff. They are associations in published data: no causal effect of any intervention and no cost are estimated.
  • S3 and S4 describe the 2022 treatment cohort and S5 the 2021 MDR/RR-TB cohort (ITR 2024); S2 counts bacteriologically confirmed patients without a valid rapid rifampicin result.
